Common questions.

How does difficulty work?

Choose Easy, Normal or Hard. Each level has four computer opponents, and one of them is picked at random each game. Easy opponents miss things; Hard opponents calculate several moves ahead.

Can I choose my opponent?

No, the surprise is part of the fun. You'll see who you're playing, their level and a little about them at the top of the board. Rematch plays the same opponent again with the colors swapped.

How do I move a piece?

Tap one of your pieces, then tap a highlighted square. Dots mark empty squares and rings mark captures. When a pawn reaches the far side, you choose what it becomes.

Why did the game end in a draw?

Chess Night declares draws automatically: stalemate, the same position three times, fifty moves each with no capture or pawn move, or too few pieces left to checkmate.

Can I take back a move?

Yes. Tap the arrow at the top of the screen. Against the computer it takes back your move and its reply. Once a game ends the result stands, so your scores stay honest.

Does it work with VoiceOver?

Yes. Each square reads its name and piece. Double-tap a piece to hear where it can go, then double-tap a square. Every move is announced in plain words. Larger text and Reduce Motion are supported too.

Is my game saved?

Yes. Chess Night saves after every move, so you can close the app and pick up where you left off. Deleting the app also deletes the saved game.

Do I need internet?

No. Chess Night works fully offline, and the computer opponents run on your iPhone.

Ads or purchases?

None. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts.

Can I play a friend?

Yes. Choose 2 Players on the start screen and type your names. You share one iPhone, and the board turns after each move so the player to move sees it from their side. You can switch turning off.

Does it keep score?

Yes. Tap Scores on the start screen to see your wins, losses and draws at each difficulty, and between each pair of two-player names. Reset clears them.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes, with the Sound effects switch on the start screen. Sounds also stay quiet when your iPhone's Ring/Silent switch is set to silent.

Which devices?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

How do I learn the rules?

During a game, tap the book icon at the top of the screen. How to Play explains how every piece moves, the special moves and the draws.
